# Scene image prompts

Prompts for generating the game's scene images with an AI image tool
(e.g. Microsoft Copilot / Bing Image Creator, Google Gemini, ChatGPT).

## How to use

1. For each scene below, paste the **style block** followed by the **scene description**.
2. Ask for a **wide landscape image (16:9)** if the tool lets you choose.
3. Generate a few versions and keep the one you like best.
4. Save it with the file name shown (e.g. `village.png`) and upload it to Claude.

Tip: generate the first image, and if you like its style, tell the tool
"same style as before" for the next ones. That keeps the set consistent.

## Style block (paste this before every scene)

> Detailed hand-drawn graphite pencil sketch on white paper, fantasy concept
> art for a storybook adventure game. Fine confident linework, light
> cross-hatching for shading, visible loose construction lines, lots of
> texture detail in foliage, stone and fabric. Almost entirely monochrome
> grey pencil, with ONE soft watercolour accent colour only where stated.
> Whimsical, slightly rounded, charming character proportions. No text,
> no border, no signature. Wide 16:9 landscape composition.

**About the hero:** the player chooses the hero's name and gender, so the
hero is always shown **small, from behind, or hooded in a travelling cloak**,
so it could be anyone.

## Cast reference (to keep characters consistent)

- **The hero:** young apprentice mage, hooded travelling cloak, short wooden staff, satchel.
- **Elder Maren:** elderly woman in flowing white robes with a deep hood, kind lined face.
- **Captain Vessa Thorne:** sky pirate in her thirties, long battered flight coat, goggles on her forehead, wild braided hair.
- **Pip:** a rukka, a huge flightless cream-and-gold riding bird, round and fluffy, with a ridiculous curly head crest and big expressive eyes, wearing a leather harness.
- **Malgareth:** tall gaunt sorcerer-king in layered black robes, a tall jagged crown and a long staff.
- **The Wandering Gale:** a patched wooden airship hanging under a long sagging balloon, with propellers and lots of rigging.
